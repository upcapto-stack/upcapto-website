FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is Upcapto?

Upcapto is a software company that builds operating systems for whole businesses. Each product runs a part of your business, and Kitty, our AI, works across all of them on one shared data core.

Do I have to buy everything at once?

No. Start with the one product you need today and switch on the rest when you are ready. Because everything shares one core, there is nothing to migrate or re-enter later.

What is “one core”, and why does it matter?

Every Upcapto product reads and writes the same database and customer records. A sale, a stock move, a payroll entry and a support ticket all stay in sync automatically, so your numbers always agree.

Which products are live right now?

Hind Workforce OS (HR and payroll) and Kitty are live in production today. Ambarion OS for restaurants is about 80% built, OneOS for optical (sold as Opticommerce) about 50%, and AI Calling Agent, GMB Manager and Market Radar are in build or scoping.

Is my data safe?

Your data is yours. It lives in one secure cloud, is backed up automatically, and is never shared across businesses. Enterprise plans add data-residency and access-control options.

Will it work with the tools I already use?

Yes. Products connect to the tools and aggregators you rely on, for example Zomato and Swiggy for restaurants or Google Business Profile for reputation, and Kitty can automate the work between them.

How do I get started?

Book a walkthrough. We map your operations onto the platform, start you on one product, and bring the rest online as you grow.
