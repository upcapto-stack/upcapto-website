FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is Upcapto?

Upcapto is a software, automation and AI company. We build and sell our own products for running a business, and we build custom software, websites and automations for any business. One team, everything under one roof.

Do you build custom software, or only sell products?

Both. Our products are ready to switch on today, and when off-the-shelf does not fit, we build custom software, websites and automations around your exact workflow. If it can be solved with software, we can build it.

Do I have to buy everything at once?

No. Start with the one product you need today and add the rest when you are ready. Because our products share one core, there is nothing to migrate or re-enter later.

What is “one core”, and why does it matter?

Every Upcapto product reads and writes the same database and customer records. A sale, a stock move, a payroll entry and a support ticket all stay in sync automatically, so your numbers always agree.

Which products are live right now?

All seven are live in production today: Etelios (retail and business OS, optical first), Rasoi Mise (restaurants), Hind Workforce OS (HR and payroll), Kitty (personal AI companion), Vaani (AI voice calling), Maan Samman (reputation and Google Business Profile) and Prabhav (all-in-one marketing suite). Etelios runs a 22-outlet optical chain pan-India, Vaani runs across three of our own companies, and Maan Samman runs across two large retail chains.

Is my data safe?

Your data is yours. It lives in one secure cloud, is backed up automatically, and is never shared across businesses. Enterprise plans add data-residency and access-control options.

Will it work with the tools I already use?

Yes. Products connect to the tools and aggregators you rely on, for example Zomato and Swiggy for restaurants or Google Business Profile for reputation, and Kitty can automate the work between them.

How do I get started?

Book a walkthrough. We map your operations onto the platform, start you on one product, and bring the rest online as you grow.
