# Upcapto — Website Copy Deck

Positioning, headlines, punchlines and body copy for the Upcapto marketing site.
Written benefit-first: every line says what it does, who it's for, and the outcome.
No empty superlatives ("#1", "world-class"), no buzzword soup. Specific over clever.

---

## 1. Brand positioning

**What Upcapto is:** a software, automation and AI company. It builds and sells its
own products for running a business, AND builds custom software, websites and
automations for any business. Everything under one roof.

**The one-liner (elevator):**
> Upcapto builds the software your business runs on: ready-made products, custom
> builds, websites and automation, with AI in all of it, from one team.

**Two sides of the business:**
1. **Products** — our own SaaS, running real businesses today (optical, F&B, workforce, AI).
2. **Services** — custom software, websites, automation and AI integration for any business.

**Who it's for:** any business with a problem software can solve. We don't pick a
vertical; if it can be automated or built, we build it.

**Proof point (true):** Upcapto runs and automates **2 of its own brands** end to
end on its software. We run it before we sell it.

**Voice:** corporate but confident and trustworthy. Plain, specific, no hype. Name
the problem, then the fix. Sound like an established company, not a startup draft.

**Taglines (pick one for ads / footer):**
- Software, automation and AI, under one roof.
- The software your business runs on.
- Products you can buy. Software we build for you.
- One partner for every operation.

## 1b. What we do (capability pillars)
- **SaaS products** — ready-made software you buy, switch on, and run from day one.
- **Custom software** — bespoke systems built around your exact workflow.
- **Websites & web apps** — modern sites and apps that run real operations.
- **Automation & AI** — automate repetitive work, wire AI across your tools.

## 10. FAQ

- **What exactly is Upcapto?** A software company that builds operating systems for whole businesses. Each product runs a part of your business, and Kitty, our AI, works across all of them on one shared data core.
- **Do I have to buy everything at once?** No. Start with the one product you need today and switch on the rest when you are ready. Because everything shares one core, there is nothing to migrate or re-enter later.
- **What is "one core", and why does it matter?** Every product reads and writes the same database and customer records. A sale, a stock move, a payroll entry and a support ticket all stay in sync automatically, so your numbers always agree.
- **Which products are live right now?** All seven are live in production today: Etelios (retail and business OS, optical first), Rasoi Mise (restaurants), Hind Workforce OS (HR and payroll), Kitty (personal AI companion), Vaani (AI voice calling), Maan Samman (reputation and Google Business Profile) and Prabhav (all-in-one marketing suite). Etelios runs a 22-outlet optical chain pan-India, Vaani runs across three of our own companies, and Maan Samman runs across two large retail chains.
- **Is my data safe?** Your data is yours. It lives in one secure cloud, is backed up automatically, and is never shared across businesses. Enterprise plans add data-residency and access-control options.
- **Will it work with the tools I already use?** Yes. Products connect to the tools and aggregators you rely on (Zomato and Swiggy, Google Business Profile, etc.), and Kitty can automate the work between them.
- **How do I get started?** Book a walkthrough. We map your operations onto the platform, start you on one product, and bring the rest online as you grow.
